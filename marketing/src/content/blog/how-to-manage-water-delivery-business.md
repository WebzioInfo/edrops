Getting pure, reliable 20L drinking water delivered to your home or office is easier than ever with eDrops. Instead of making daily phone calls or dealing with cash, you manage your water orders through a simple mobile application.

eDrops is purpose-built for seamless 20L water jar delivery: choose your delivery schedule, pay securely via prepaid wallet, and enjoy doorstep jar swaps on time.

### How It Works:

- **Flexible Delivery Schedules**: Choose one-time delivery or set up a recurring plan for daily, alternate-day, or weekly drops.
- **Prepaid Digital Wallet**: Top up with UPI or card. Each delivery deducts automatically upon doorstep arrival with an instant digital receipt.
- **Doorstep Jar Swap**: Your delivery partner brings clean, sealed 20L water jars and collects the empty containers right at your door.
- **Total Control**: Pause deliveries when traveling, skip a drop, or add extra jars for family gatherings directly from your phone.
